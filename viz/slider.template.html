This is a toy. It is not a forecast and not a study result. Every input other than the three controls is held fixed, and those constants are simplifications: the lowest temperature is always the highest minus 8 °C, and the mean is the midpoint of the two; the three preceding days are assumed identical to this one, so every lagged input equals its same-day value; the seven-day totals for precipitation, rain and snow are seven times this day's value, and the seven-day freeze–thaw count is seven times this day's indicator; rain is set equal to total precipitation, so the snow control adds snow without removing any rain; the maximum wind speed is held at __WIND__ km/h, the median over the training days; and the trend covariate is held at the last training day, __TLAST__. Real days do not behave like this. The models behind it are the same WEATHER models the study fitted, with the same fixed hyperparameters, trained on days up to that date and never on the held-out year.
